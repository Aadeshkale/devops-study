
## ML OPS Notes

##  1. What is MLOPS

* MLOps means applying DevOps principles and practices to the ML lifecycle, including continuous integration, continuous training, deployment, and monitoring of ML models 
---

##  2. What are the phases of MLOPS

* **Development & Experimentation**: Develop ML code, experiment with different models and features, and track experiments.
* **Data Management & Versioning**: Collect, validate, process, and version datasets and features (ETL). 
* **Model Training**: Train models using versioned data and automate the training pipeline while tracking model artifacts and parameters.
* **Model Evaluation & Validation**: Evaluate model performance against defined metrics and thresholds before deployment.
* **Model Deployment / Serving**: Deploy the validated model for online or batch predictions and automate deployment using CI/CD.
* **Monitoring & Maintenance**: Monitor model performance, data/model drift, and infrastructure, and retrain the model when required.
